01 · The problem

Industrial energy is fragmented by default

A single plant runs a grid connection, a solar plant, a battery, diesel backup, chillers, compressors and production lines — each with its own controller, its own vendor portal and its own data model. Nothing sees the whole picture, so nothing can optimise it.

03 · Energy Nexus

One model. Every asset. Continuous decisions.

Energy Nexus resolves five questions at all times — and every screen in the OS answers one of them.

04 · Digital twin

A machine-readable model of the plant, reconciled continuously

Not a decorative 3D factory. The twin holds topology, asset models, load, production, commercial terms, environmental conditions, grid state, carbon and — critically — the constraint register the customer signs at commissioning.

05 · AI energy agent

A constrained agent that explains itself

Every site gets a persistent software entity that owns its energy decisions, quantifies the value of each one, and escalates anything it should not do alone.

Every action carries its reasoning

Autonomy is earned, not assumed

Two decisions are permanently human. Diesel dispatch and any regulated energy transaction stay recommend-and-approve for the life of the product, regardless of autonomy tier. Both carry statutory and counterparty exposure the customer holds, not us.

07 · Industrial network

Clusters first, not consumers

The unit of value is the cluster, not the site. Dense industrial belts give shared infrastructure, shared counterparties and reference customers who talk to each other.

08 · Long-term vision

Toward an energy internet

Electricity, heat, cooling, storage, flexibility and eventually hydrogen, coordinated across industrial ecosystems under a common protocol.

This is a long-term vision, not an operating capability. Energy trading and multi-energy routing depend on regulatory change and are shown throughout the platform as simulation, pilot or regulation-dependent. Nothing here represents a live public electricity trading network.

09 · Roadmap

Nine rungs, each independently saleable

The company does not attempt the energy internet on day one. Each rung generates the data, the trust and the installed base the next one needs.

Horizons follow the staged strategy in the business case. The Energy Router and flexibility aggregation tracks run in parallel from roughly month 26 onward.

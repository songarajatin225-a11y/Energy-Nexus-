Methodology

BRSR export

One row per site plus an estate total, structured for the energy and emissions fields of SEBI's Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (Principle 6): electricity from renewable and non-renewable sources, Scope 2 emissions and intensity, with the factor and method stated on every row.

Every row is marked SIMULATED — not assured. The export is a working draft for a sustainability team, not a filing.

What each screen does, which keyboard shortcuts exist, and — importantly — what is simulated.

This is a demonstration prototype. All telemetry, sites, assets, savings and market records are simulated from a deterministic dataset. Nothing connects to real plant equipment, and no figure represents an actual customer.
